Savings

How the month-end sweep works. Once a month has ended, the system posts one automatic Savings entry equal to income − expenses − savings already recorded that month, so a finished month's savings always equal its surplus. The sweep is idempotent: it updates the same entry instead of adding another one, and it runs by itself whenever data changes. The month you are currently in is left alone until it ends.
